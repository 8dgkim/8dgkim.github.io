In early 2023, I designed, built, and launched a high-power rocket on an H-class motor at the MDRA Red Glare event in Maryland, USA, and earned Level 1 High Power Rocketry certification from the National Association of Rocketry (NAR).

## Design and build

Flight dynamics were modeled in the OpenRocket simulator, and the airframe was designed in Fusion 360. Custom components were 3D printed while precision parts were laser cut. The parachute deployment system was modified before launch.
